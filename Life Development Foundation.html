Who we are

Empowering individuals.
Inspiring change.

Think of LDF as an online town square: learn a skill, find a trusted tutor, get guided to the right help, or support a neighbour. Everything is connected.

🔭
OUR VISION

Spirituality and good attitudes, alongside knowledge and skills.

A positive, thriving community grows when character and capability grow together.

🚀
OUR MISSION

Spread knowledge and run workshops that inspire change.

Helping people find peace of mind and raising responsible citizens who contribute to their country.

Step by step

Everything we do, in 6 steps

From the first hello to measurable results, this is the journey every person and partner follows.

👂

Listen & consult

We ask communities, learners, volunteers and host organisations what they actually need before we build.

Result: services shaped by real feedback
🔗

Connect

People join the platform free and are matched with learning, support, tutors and volunteers.

Result: one open community
🎓

Learn

Free resources, structured courses, verified tutors and workshops in English, Sinhala and Tamil.

Result: new skills and certificates
🍲

Support

LDF Hope delivers food and practical help with receipt-backed auditing and dignity at its core.

Result: help that is provable
🧭

Guide

Pathfinder points each person to the right learning, health, legal or professional support.

Result: nobody left unsure where to turn
📈

Grow & report

Income is reinvested and partners receive clear impact and financial reports.

Result: trust and long-term growth
