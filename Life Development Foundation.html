In plain words

What is LDF, in 30 seconds?

Think of LDF as a community “town square” online. You can learn a skill, find a trusted tutor, get guided to the right help, or support a neighbour in need. Everything is connected, so one visit can lead to many opportunities.

What’s in it for me?

Learn, get help, give back

Free to join. Use free resources any time, and pay only for optional extras.

  • Free learning materials and community workshops
  • Book verified tutors and professionals
  • Be guided to the right support by Pathfinder

Fund impact you can see

Sponsor the whole platform or one programme, with clear reports in return.

  • Approved logo placement and partner profile
  • Impact reports with agreed, measurable outcomes
  • Employee volunteering and mentoring options

Share your skills, earn fairly

Offer paid sessions or volunteer your time to the community.

  • Run paid courses, tutoring and consultations
  • LDF takes only an agreed commission on completed bookings
  • Free workshops build your reputation

Tools to run your projects

Charities, schools and groups can use the platform to organise work.

  • Publish projects and manage volunteers
  • Host workshops and team collaboration
  • Optional advanced dashboards by subscription
