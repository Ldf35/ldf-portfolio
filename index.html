Why LDF exists

Not just a platform.
A connected community.

LDF began in 2019 with a simple ambition: to contribute something meaningful to society by developing people's thinking, knowledge, abilities and opportunities. The long-term vision is to bring different forms of community participation together in one place.

🌱

Learn and develop

Discover knowledge, practise skills, receive guidance and keep moving forward.

🤝

Support one another

Connect people, volunteers and organisations around meaningful community projects.

🌍

Create opportunities

Make it easier to discover people, services, partnerships and opportunities to contribute.

A possible intelligent layer

How could AI support LDF?

The long-term concept is an intelligent assistant that helps people navigate the ecosystem. It could help users discover learning resources, understand project information and find relevant next steps.

01
Understand
Listen to what the person is trying to achieve.
02
Connect
Identify relevant LDF sections, resources or people.
03
Guide
Explain possible next steps and useful options.
04
Follow up
Help users continue their learning or community journey.

Concept demonstration only. This portfolio guide is scripted and does not use a live AI model or access personal user data.

Working together

There is room for everyone.

LDF aims to create meaningful ways for individuals and organisations to participate, contribute and collaborate.

🎓 Universities and education

Explore learning collaborations, workshops, student participation and educational projects.

💻 Technology partners

Help improve the platform, develop useful tools and strengthen the digital experience.

💚 Charities and social enterprises

Explore shared projects, community support and transparent delivery opportunities.

🙌 Volunteers and communities

Bring local knowledge, time, skills and energy to community activities.

🏢 Businesses and professionals

Share expertise, services, learning opportunities and responsible marketplace ideas.

🌍 Strategic supporters

Help shape the vision, test ideas, strengthen governance and explore sustainable development.
