The idea

People should be able to give, learn and grow — not just receive.

LDF connects people, skills and organisations through a shared digital environment. Someone may join to learn, later volunteer, then lead a project or share their expertise. Organisations can find people, publish opportunities and collaborate.

Current stage

Built to move forward carefully.

LDF has an existing web platform and a developed concept. The next stage is to validate user journeys, review security and governance, test with real users and run focused pilot projects.

Development priorities

✓ Review and improve existing features

✓ Test user, volunteer and organisation journeys

✓ Establish safeguarding, privacy and data governance

✓ Define project finance and reporting controls

✓ Run measurable pilots before wider expansion

Invitation to collaborate

Looking for a strategic UK partner.

LDF is seeking an established organisation with experience in community development, education, volunteering, social innovation or digital services. A partner could help review the model, strengthen governance, connect with communities, identify suitable funding routes and co-design a focused pilot.

What LDF brings: an existing initiative, a developed digital platform, a connected service vision and a long-term commitment to social value.

What we hope to build together: a practical, accountable pilot with clear outcomes, shared responsibilities and a realistic development plan.
